Privacy Policy – Matthews Pickups

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Matthews Pickups is a private, internal application used only by Matthews Metal & Auto Recycling (Beaverton, Ontario) to manage its own vehicle pickups and bookkeeping. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

Data we access

When connected to QuickBooks Online, the app reads and writes accounting records of our own company only (customers, invoices, bills and payments) to record pickup-related invoices and payments.

How data is used and stored

Data is used solely for our internal bookkeeping. It is stored in our private database with access limited to authorized company staff. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties.

Disconnecting

The QuickBooks connection can be removed at any time from QuickBooks Online (Apps > My apps). Stored tokens are then no longer valid.
